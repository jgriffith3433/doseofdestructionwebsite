    How to play

    The basics

    1. Hits add damage. The percent under each fighter is how far the next hit sends them.
    2. Launch them off. Past the edge of the screen, that fighter loses a stock.
    3. Get back. Jump, use your air jumps, then up special (up + special) to reach the ledge.
    4. Last one standing takes the match.
