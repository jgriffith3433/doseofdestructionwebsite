    All controls

    The basics

    1. Hits add damage. The percent under each fighter is how far the next hit sends them.
    2. Launch them off. Past the edge of the screen, that fighter loses a stock.
    3. Get back. Jump, use your air jumps, then up special (up + special) to reach the ledge.
    4. On the ledge, choose. Stick up gets you up, Attack sweeps low, Shield rolls in, Jump leaps. Hang too long and you drop.
    5. Special changes with the stick. Hold a direction with Special for the side, up or down special; each fighter's are their own.
    6. Last one standing takes the match.

      Captions

      Music, Effects and Voice (announcer, commentary, story and fighter lines) are separate. Captions show the spoken lines as text; story lines still show when sound is off. Screen shake is the camera kick on hits and KOs. Rumble buzzes a controller or phone by how hard a hit lands (a short buzz, a jolt, a long one for a KO), softer with reduced motion. Touch size and opacity (touch devices) tune the on-screen stick. Quality Low draws fewer pixels and sparks, for phones; Glow is the bloom on bright art. Press M any time to turn sound on or off.
