Privacy

Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026. This covers doseofdestruction.com and the game opened from Play.

The website

Home, the blog, contact, and this page are ordinary web pages. They do not ask you to make an account. They do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The contact form does not save what you type on our server. Send opens your own email app.

The game on your device

Play loads a Unity game in the browser once a build is published. The first visit downloads the game files. Unity can keep a cache of those files on your device so the next visit starts faster. That cache stays on your device. Clear site data for doseofdestruction.com in your browser and it goes away.

Email you send

If you use the contact form, the message contains whatever you put in it, including your email address. It is used to reply. It is not added to a mailing list. Ask and that message will be deleted.

What this site does not collect

This site does not collect a headset identifier, a phone number, or a payment card. Personal information is not sold.

Children

The website does not ask for a name or an account to read it or to press Play. A parent who wants the browser copy removed can clear site data, or write to the contact address.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
