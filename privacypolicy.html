Privacy

Privacy policy

Updated October 14, 2026. This covers doseofdestruction.com and the game opened from Play.

The website

Home, the roster, and this page are ordinary web pages. They do not ask you to make an account. They do not set advertising or analytics cookies.

The game on your device

Play opens the game in the browser. It remembers your last menu choices (fighters, bot levels, rules) and which campaign chapters you have cleared, in your browser’s local storage, so you pick up where you left off. That stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clear site data for doseofdestruction.com in your browser and it goes away.

What this site does not collect

This site does not collect a headset identifier, a phone number, or a payment card. Personal information is not sold.

Children

The website does not ask for a name or an account to read it or to press Play. A parent who wants the browser copy removed can clear site data.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
