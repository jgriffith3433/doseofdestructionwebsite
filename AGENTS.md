## Online

Online is always on (docs/online.md) and runs entirely on **Hawkes Net** (project `doseofdestruction`, Hawkes Gen
`docs/net.md`; the SDK is the package `@hawkesgen/net` in package.json, from the public hawkesnet-js repo by tag: update by bumping the tag): every page joins one lobby room on load
(no codes), the main menu shows "N online" and who, **Find match** queues (the player keeps playing anything meanwhile
under a "Searching for opponent…" chip), a pair is made by a deterministic rule plus a handshake, and the two meet in a
pair room that starts peer to peer and falls back to Hawkes Net's relay. The fight runs the sim through rollback
netcode with binary input packets sent as Hawkes Net express frames (immediate, unreliable), with a desync hash. Only
doseofdestruction.com uses the live rooms and leaderboard on net.hawkesgen.ai; beta and localhost are test (`net/hawkes.ts`), and the beta site plays on beta Hawkes Net (hawkesnet.hawkesgen.ai: Studio's page script sets `HAWKES_NET_ENDPOINT`). The code is
`src/game/net/` and `src/game/ui/online.ts`. **Profile** is a name and a fighter picture (localStorage `dod.profile`); online you fight as
your picture. Every finished online match fires the match-result event (`net/results.ts` `onMatchResult`,
`window` `dod:match-result`). **Leaderboard** (menu): both players sign each online result with their profile key
(localStorage `dod.profile.key`), it is kept as a Hawkes Net record, and every page builds the same Elo table from the
signed results (`net/ratings.ts` rules, `net/board.ts` + `net/records.ts` store and cache, `ui/leaderboard.ts`). `?room=<id>` is a private test
lobby (and its own leaderboard). Network errors go to
`console.error("[dod-online] …")`. `npm test` covers pairing, the rollback and the rating rules; `node tools/online-live.test.ts`
runs two players over a Hawkes Net started in the process from the Gen checkout (direct link; `NO_LINK=1` for the
relay), signed results included. After a change to Gen's `clients/js/hawkes-net*.mjs`, recopy them here.

## Auto quality

The renderer governs its own frame time (`render/autoQuality.ts` pure state, wired in `render/renderer.ts` `autoTick`, no
separate watchdog). Over the first 12 s of a match's play it takes the median frame time of 0.8 s windows of calm frames
(never hit-stop or punch frames); over 23.5 ms it steps down: 1 render scale 0.75, 2 glow off, 3 post on its Low path +
parallax/haze layers hidden (`Placed.culled`), 4 scale 0.5 (levels that change nothing on this device are skipped; very slow
takes two steps). Steps up happen only at the next match start, one level, only with 56+ fps headroom, and never again after
one failed. The first step down shows one toast ever (`dod.gfx.auto-toast`); the level is kept in `dod.gfx.auto`. Quality or Glow
pressed by hand in Settings (`setGraphics(..., true)` -> `dod.gfx.hand`, or an old saved choice that differs from the device
default) switches it off for good. Scenario `auto-quality` (simulated slow device via `autoSetup({slowMs})`; every other
scenario turns auto quality off); metrics `autoLevel`, `autoSettleMs`, `autoFpsBefore/After`; `dod:auto-quality` event; unit test `test/autoQuality.test.ts`.

## Library (Hawkes Gen)

Dev Tools → **Library** shows every fighter in Hawkes Gen's library (`hawkesgen/docs/library.md`), the same one
Studio, the other products and Claude use. Each slot keeps every version:
- the slots: concept, reference, side, `pose/<name>`, `clip/<name>`
- what you can do: pick, discard, note, revise with feedback, make more takes

The recipes are DoD's tuned settings, in `hawkesgen/presets/doseofdestruction.json` under `kinds.fighter`.

`hawkes.library.json` says which `AllTheCharacters` file is which slot.
- **Import from AllTheCharacters** brings folders in. It's safe to run again.
- A pick is written back to the fighter folder ("Write picks back", or **Write back**), so the game and the other
  tabs keep reading the folders.
- From a terminal: `node C:/Projects/hawkesgen/bin/hawkesgen-folders.mjs status|import|export hawkes.library.json [--only Break] [--dry]`.

The web components are a copy of Hawkes Gen's (`src/devtools/hawkesgen/`; copy them again when Gen's change). They
call Gen through this dev server (`/api/dev/gen/v1/...`), which adds the key.

## Playtests

The game implements the Hawkes playtest contract v1 (`window.hawkesPlaytest`; spec in
`hawkesstudio/docs/playtest.md`). The code is `src/game/playtest/`: `hawkes-playtest.js` is a copy of
`hawkesstudio/clients/web/hawkes-playtest.js` (update it from there, don't edit it here), and `dod.ts` has
the scenarios. The bot plays through real input: menus are clicked, pause is the Esc key, and in a match
the CPU bot (`src/game/ai/bot.ts`, level 9) drives your fighter through a virtual pad that the input layer
(`input/devices.ts`) reads like a gamepad. It never edits sim state. Nothing runs unless a scenario starts.

**Story scenes** (studio quality rule 6e/6f/6g: story through play, not text). An episode's intro and outro play
live in the arena (`src/game/ui/scene.ts`, phase `scene` in main.ts), never on a text screen: after the VS card the
camera opens wide on the stage with the studio crowd and a broadcast sting, the episode's story card slides in as the
show's lower third ("Tonight"), the fighters drop onto the stage one by one (dust, a thud, a small shake), then each
line of `intro` plays: the camera eases onto the speaker if they're in the fight (with a little bounce), or holds a
slow wide push for the host and narrator (their head shows by the line), the voice take plays over ducked music, and
the text is one short line at a time (`campaign/sceneText.ts`: at most 10 words, timed to the take or to reading
speed). Then Ready, Go. A won episode's `outro` plays the same way after Game!, on the winner, with the "Signing off"
card, before the results. Next (Enter, Space) moves on; Skip, Esc or Start ends it. The announcer's and Blog Man's
captions also show one short line at a time. Procedural sounds (`src/game/synth.ts`: crowd, cheer, whoosh, sting,
alarm, thud, plus continuous stage beds) cover moments with no recorded file: stage event warnings and starts, KOs,
Game!, arrivals. Every bout plays a layered bed (ambience + theme + intensity lift on last stock / ≥100%, final-KO
sting) from `content/stage-beds.json`.

Every page load starts behind the studio's full-screen Tap to play prompt (`#tap-to-play` in `Game/index.html`; its
inline script unlocks sound even before the module loads); scenarios tap it first. Sound settings are the studio
standard: `localStorage["hawkes.sound"]` = `{ on, music, sfx }` (`src/game/soundSettings.ts`); Effects also drives voices.

Every match the hook watches is also an animation-over-time trace (`src/game/sim/trace.ts`, studio quality rule 5):
per frame, each fighter's action, grounded flag and speed. A bug problem when an air state (jump/fall, air dodge,
helpless) is held on the ground for over 0.3 s (the landing never fired), a ground state is held in the air for over
0.3 s, a fighter walks in place or idles while sliding at walking speed for over 0.3 s, or a timed state lasts over 5 s
(no exit); event `anim-trace` at each match end. `npm test` runs the same trace on every bot match. Walk clips play by
ground covered, not by the clock (`render/anims.ts` `walkFrame`: playback = speed / the clip's natural speed, which is
the fighter's full walking speed at the clip's fps), so slow walks don't slide.

Reported: metrics `kos`, `falls`, `damageDealt`, `damageTaken` (percent), `matchSeconds`, `stocksLeft`,
`wins`/`losses`, `chaptersCleared`, plus fps; events `match-start`, `ko`, `match-end`, `chapter`,
`chapter-cleared`; problems for missing images, clip sheets, sounds and voice files (404s), fighters without
clips or with stand-in clips, unrecorded voice lines, unpainted stage layers, stages without music, NaN
positions, a fighter out of bounds without a KO, a stopped match clock, 30 s with no hits, and moves that
don't come out.

The landing page speaks the contract too (`site/playtest-bridge.js`), because Studio's playtests open the project's
address, which is the landing page: on `start()` (or `/?playtest=<id>`) it opens `Game/` in a full-screen frame, passes
on the query string (`room`, `peer`), starts the scenario in the game's own contract and passes `state()`, `report()`,
`stop()` and `input()` through. Its scenario list is copied from `src/game/playtest/scenarios.ts` by
`node tools/playtest-bridge.mjs`; `npm test` fails while the copy is stale (new scenarios or chapters).

Run one: start the dev server and open `http://localhost:8089/Game/?playtest=smoke` (also `&seed=2`,
`&speed=2` for up to 4x sim speed). In the console, `hawkesPlaytest.state()` while it runs and
`hawkesPlaytest.report()` when it ends. Hawkes Gen's `playtest.run` does the same and records it.
Headless Chromium renders in software here (~6–10 fps), so the sim runs at about half speed and the
campaign gets through 2–3 chapters in its budget.

## Making content (agents)

New stages, fighters, lines and music are made end to end by agents with the skills in `.claude/skills/`
(Claude Code loads them by themselves): `dod-stage` (concept → design → painted layers → `stages.json` +
`modes.json` → music recipe → playtest; headless through `tools/maps/make.mjs`), `dod-fighter` (side view → poses →
clips → `fighters.json`), `dod-voice-music`. Every generated file goes through Hawkes Gen and, when it's a file the
game ships, through `hawkes.assets.json`. GPU work is priced first (`hawkesgen_estimate`, or `make.mjs --estimate`)
and needs J's OK. A fighter's smash and special hitboxes are fitted to its clip sheets by `node tools/fit-hitboxes.mjs` (`--check` for 0 misses; run it after any clip change). Content playtests target the new content with scenario options: `{"stage": "<id>"}` or
`{"fighter": "<id>"}` on `match`/`smoke` (second line of `.hawkes/scenario.txt`).

**Post chain** (`render/post.ts`, `render/grade.ts`): one full-screen pass on the match camera (HUD, captions and touch controls are DOM, so never graded). Each stage's `grade` in `content/stages.json` (lift, gamma, gain, saturation, tint, tintAmount) is baked by `node tools/make-luts.mjs` into `stages/<id>/lut.png` (16x16x16 strip; `--check` is in `npm test`): rerun it after editing a grade. High adds light grain and a 4-frame chromatic aberration + exposure punch on heavy hits and KOs; Low is LUT + vignette only; the farthest backdrop layer is softened on High (`stageArt.ts` `softenFar`). Bloom is the Glow layer (emissive only, its setting unchanged). Scenario `post-chain`.
