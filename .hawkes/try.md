1. Open the beta game on a phone (or browser devtools at 390x844, then 844x390) and tap to play.
2. Main menu: every entry is a full-height tappable row, the "Tonight" ticker is slim at the bottom (sideways, the menu is two columns).

4. Watch the VS screen (both name cards on screen), then finish a match: results text fits and buttons are easy to hit.
