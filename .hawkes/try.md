1. Open the game on beta, press start, then open Settings from the main menu. You should see Quality, Glow and Show FPS. On a desktop it should say High, On, Off. On a phone it should say Low, Off, Off.
2. Start a Versus fight. Press Esc (or the pause button), open Settings, and turn on Show FPS. A small "NN FPS" box appears in the bottom-right corner.
3. Turn Glow off and then on. The bright edges of the art stop glowing and come back, and the fight never reloads.
4. Switch Quality to Low. The picture gets a little softer and the sparks thinner. Press Done, resume, and reload the page. Your choices are still there.
