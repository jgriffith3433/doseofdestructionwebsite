How a match goes

A stock is a life. Leave the blast zone and that stock is gone. Last one still standing takes the match.

01

Take damage

Every hit adds to your percent. The number is how far the next clean hit can send you.

02

Get launched

The stage has edges. A strong hit at a high percent carries you off them.

03

Lose a stock

Past the blast zone, that life is gone. Run out of stocks and the match is over.
