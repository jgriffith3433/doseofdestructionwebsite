# Open Mic: Exit Punk vs Shutup Bot — story

**Logline.** A nobody with no record walks onto Open Mic and silences the robot whose whole job is silencing people, using the only thing he owns: his skateboard.

**Exit Punk** wants a record: to be somebody on the card. He likes the bot's flat wall and pokes it with his mouth; cocky, amused, sure of himself.
**Shutup Bot** wants quiet. He regards the kid as noise; flat, servo-stiff, unmoved, and honest to a fault, so he even admits his own gag is true.

**The turn.** Exit Punk says the bot's whole thing out loud ("Saying that is your whole thing"), the bot can only answer "...Yes." (he's beaten by being right), and the kid flips the board up and swings.
**The payoff.** The bot doesn't move, then says a flat "Ow." from a dented, smoking head. The kid smirks and walks off with his board: his record, set.

**Why it matters.** It is Open Mic from the campaign: the walk-on with one skateboard, a TV show with a host, and the first proof the kid can take the card.

**Who it's for.** Anyone: a loud kid, a robot who tells people to stop talking, a hit that finally gets a reaction.
