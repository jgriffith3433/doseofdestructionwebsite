# The Board (Exit Punk origin) (J's script)

THE BOARD. The Foundry, 11:40 PM. Cast: Exit Punk (a kid, parts runner), The Mechanic (wrench, long day, no quit).

BEATS
1. HOOK (0:00): Exit Punk launches off a conveyor belt mid-air, board spinning, a bolt clutched in his fist. Wheels hit the steel floor in a spray of sparks. Whip-pan after him as he weaves through the furnace floor.
2. The Mechanic, under a half-dismantled machine, slides out on a creeper. Doesn't look up. Checks a watch the size of a plate.
MECHANIC: You're late.
EXIT PUNK: I'm eleven seconds late.
MECHANIC: Eleven seconds is how a furnace goes out.
EXIT PUNK: It's a bolt.
MECHANIC: It's THE bolt. (takes it. Then sees the board.) That deck's snapped.
EXIT PUNK: It's fine. It's held together by optimism.
MECHANIC: It's held together by me. Third time this month. Sit.
3. MONTAGE, no dialogue: the Mechanic rebuilds the deck. Extreme close-ups: welding sparks, a file on edge, wheels spun and checked by ear. Exit Punk watches, swinging his legs, trying not to look like he cares.
4. Quiet two-shot, the board finished between them.
MECHANIC: You know why you keep breaking it?
EXIT PUNK: I'm bad at stopping.
MECHANIC: You never ride it anywhere you're allowed.
EXIT PUNK: Nowhere I'm allowed to ride has an exit.
MECHANIC: (long beat) There's a window upstairs. Gets the broadcast. Whole city watching the floor at midnight. People who walk onto that stage don't come back the same. Or at all.
EXIT PUNK: Sounds like a way out.
MECHANIC: It's a way in. (hands the board back) New rule. Wheels down. Whatever you land on, wheels down.
EXIT PUNK: That's the rule?
MECHANIC: Only one I ever kept. And bring the bolt back.
5. THE RIDE: Exit Punk skates the whole length of the Foundry to the drum loop, one long unbroken Blender camera (crane-up at the end) up to the stage door. Through the grate: the midnight lights come up. The Announcer, muffled through a speaker: "Tonight, a walk-on."
6. Close-up Exit Punk, reflected stage light in his eyes. EXIT PUNK: Wheels down. He drops in. Cut to black.
7. END CARD: Dose Of Destruction. Made with Hawkes Gen · hawkesgen.ai (small).
