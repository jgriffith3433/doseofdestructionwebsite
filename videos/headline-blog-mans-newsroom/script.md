HEADLINE. Blog Man's newsroom, between nights. Exit Punk comes to complain. Blog Man hosts Season 2 and covers Exit Punk's run.

BEATS
1. HOOK (0:00): a typewriter bell and a page ripped out of the carriage. SMASH CUT to a front page: the headline types itself in huge letters. Blog Man's fist slams the desk.
BLOG MAN: Headline. 'Local Skater Defeats Large Shark. Shark Declines Comment.'
2. EXIT PUNK walks in, holding the paper like it owes him money.
EXIT PUNK: He commented. He said he needed one more bite.
BLOG MAN: A man's last words aren't a comment. They're a pull quote.
EXIT PUNK: Can you stop writing about me?
BLOG MAN: I'm on the sports desk. You are the sports.
EXIT PUNK: Shorter.
BLOG MAN: Everyone says shorter. Do you know how many words a legend gets? Sit down. I'll show you.
3. MONTAGE, the front pages of the run, each one a short playing halftone clip from the real fights, the headline stamping on over it, Blog Man narrating at speed:
BLOG MAN: Night one. Kaelen. 'Hi, Bye.' Night two. The house fell off the house. Night three, I lost count of the floors he fell, and he didn't lose count of mine.
(Exit Punk, reading over his shoulder: "That one doesn't even make sense." Blog Man: "It's the best sentence in the building.")
4. A beat of quiet. Exit Punk looks at the wall: a hundred old front pages, all different walk-ons, all with a different name. None of them are still on the card.
EXIT PUNK: Who are all these?
BLOG MAN: The card changed every night. Nobody kept the paper. (he adjusts a pin) I keep the paper.
EXIT PUNK: Why?
BLOG MAN: Because somebody should know your name after you're gone. (beat) And because the art desk makes the front-page picture in forty seconds. Hawkes Gen. Fastest coworker I have ever had. And the quietest.
5. Exit Punk takes a blank page, rolls it into the typewriter and types one line. CU on the keys. Blog Man reads it over his shoulder.
INSERT: 'STILL HERE.'
BLOG MAN: That's... two words.
EXIT PUNK: You asked for shorter.
BLOG MAN: (a long pause, then, quietly) Run it.
6. REACTION CU Blog Man, honestly moved, then he catches himself. BLOG MAN: Of course I'd have used a bigger font.
7. Pneumatic tube thunks; the page flies through the building and prints a million times as the Announcer reads it over a speaker: "Still here."
8. END CARD: Dose Of Destruction. Made with Hawkes Gen · hawkesgen.com (small).
