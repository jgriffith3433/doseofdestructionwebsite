# Drop In (Exit Punk and Pyro Singer, the music video)

Wide, 1920x1080, ~2:00. J's script, as written:

DROP IN. Rainbow Full Pipe. Pyro Singer opens every show and closes every show. Tonight the network wants highlights, scored in KOs. She is tired of being the opener. Exit Punk has never sung in his life.

BEATS
1. HOOK (0:00): a crash cymbal and Pyro Singer exhales a ribbon of fire into the dark, which lights the pipe from end to end in rainbow colours. CU her eyes, mic in hand.
PYRO SINGER: I sing the opener. I sing the closer. You're the chorus.
EXIT PUNK: I don't sing.
PYRO SINGER: Nobody asked.
2. THE SONG begins. VERSE 1, Pyro Singer, performing to camera between the pipe's ramps:
They hand me a light and they tell me when to burn.
Opener, closer, I wait for my turn.
Every night the same song, a different crowd.
I've been loud so long I forgot how loud.
(Visual: close-ups of her face as the performance cracks, from showy to honest. Fire ripples through her hair on the high notes.)
3. EXIT PUNK drops in. Down the pipe, orbit camera, board sparks, his shadow stretching across the rainbow. First chorus, they take it together:
Drop in, drop in, don't look at the floor.
Fire on the way down and a little bit more.
Drop in, drop in, it's the only way through.
Nobody came for the plan. They came for you.
(On "plan" Exit Punk is a half-step flat. REACTION CU Pyro Singer, one eyebrow lifting. REACTION CU Exit Punk: "I know.")
4. VERSE 2, Exit Punk, spoken more than sung, nervous:
I've got one skateboard and no record to show.
I can't hold a note but I know where to go.
Don't ask me for rhythm, I've got speed instead.
I just don't stop till the thing's been said.
(While this plays, the fight happens in the pipe: he knocks out one rival, two, three. Slow-motion KO impacts, Pyro Singer's fire as the exclamation mark. The crowd roars, the scoreboard ticks to 3.)
5. BRIDGE: everything cuts out but a single sustained note and the pipe. Both on the lip of the biggest ramp, a long shot from behind. PYRO SINGER: Three already? The song was supposed to be longer. EXIT PUNK: Sing it again.
6. FINAL CHORUS, both, full band, the whole arena, fire, confetti, a drop from the top of the pipe in one long crane-down. Everyone cheers: Got Ur Back crying, Chooser jumping, Blog Man scribbling.
7. After, quiet. The pipe empty, embers.
PYRO SINGER: You were flat.
EXIT PUNK: I'm a chorus.
PYRO SINGER: (a small smile) Stay flat.
8. END CARD: Dose Of Destruction. Made with Hawkes Gen · hawkesgen.com (small).
