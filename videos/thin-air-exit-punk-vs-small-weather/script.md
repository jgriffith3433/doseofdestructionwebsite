## J's script
THIN AIR. The desert stage. Small Weather can control the weather and feels like a god. He was born in the first wave of AI video, when everyone stopped to watch. Then something newer came along. (His origin is from J's canon; the feeling below is the proposed story.)

BEATS
1. HOOK (0:00): a pickup truck lifts off the sand, silent, slow, weightless, then a rain drop rises past the camera. Exit Punk, board in hand, looks up. Gravity just left.
2. WS low angle: Small Weather descends through thunderheads, lightning coiling up his arms.
SMALL WEATHER: The Orchestrator asked for drama, so I brought the weather.
EXIT PUNK: Is that allowed?
SMALL WEATHER: It's a broadcast.
3. ACTION: Exit Punk pushes off the ground into the low-gravity air, ollies off the floating truck, grinds along a streak of lightning. Small Weather throws bolts, and each one is a flash cut to an impact in close-up: sparks, the board's tail scorched, a shockwave through the rain. Exit Punk whip-pans through the debris, a rolling fight in the open sky.
4. THE HIT: Exit Punk lands a kick; Small Weather spins and the clouds recoil. SLOW MOTION, CU on Small Weather's glasses cracking.
SMALL WEATHER: Huh. You fall like you mean it.
5. THE QUIET: the storm settles. They sit on a floating rock, rain hanging in the air around them like lights, the sun on a small dune.
SMALL WEATHER: When I first showed up, people stood outside and watched me for an hour. A whole hour. For a cloud.
EXIT PUNK: What happened?
SMALL WEATHER: A newer cloud.
EXIT PUNK: ...That's rough.
SMALL WEATHER: It rains harder when no one's looking. Don't tell the forecast.
(reaction CU: Exit Punk, a gentle, serious look. He doesn't say "I'm sorry". He says nothing, and that is the kindness.)
6. Small Weather stands, offers him one last bolt of lightning in his palm like a gift, then lets it go. The rock sinks. The truck, the rain, everything starts to come down.
EXIT PUNK: Turn the gravity back on on your way out.
SMALL WEATHER: It was only ever me holding it up.
7. THE FALL: crane-down as everything lands in slow motion. Exit Punk's wheels hit the sand, wheels down, a long skid, dust and last raindrops. He looks up; Small Weather is gone, but a single patch of sky is still raining on a little cloud that follows him home.
