# Thin Air: story

**Logline**: A god of weather drops gravity on a desert to put on a show for a broadcast; Exit Punk fights him through the floating sky, and learns the god is only loud because he was once the thing everyone stopped watching, so he gives him the one kindness he can: silence, and a landing.

**Exit Punk**: wants to get through the segment on his board. Wary, then moved by Small Weather; says nothing and that is his kindness. Attitude: deadpan, steady.
**Small Weather**: wants to be watched. Feels like a god, is afraid of being replaced ("a newer cloud"). Attitude: smooth, theatrical, rueful underneath.

**Turn**: after the hit, "You fall like you mean it", Small Weather stops performing and tells the truth. **Payoff**: he lets the gravity go ("It was only ever me holding it up"); Exit Punk lands wheels down and a small cloud follows him home.
