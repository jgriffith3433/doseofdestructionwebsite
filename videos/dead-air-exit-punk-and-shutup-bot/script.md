# Dead Air (Exit Punk and Shutup Bot, stuck elevator): J script (wide), 2026-10-22

DEAD AIR. A service elevator under the Foundry, 11:51 PM, nine minutes to showtime. Exit Punk and Shutup Bot, stuck. Bot never speaks. This is before they fight.

BEATS
1. HOOK (0:00): the elevator lurches and dies. Lights out. Emergency red. CU Exit Punk's face lit from below, mid-word.
EXIT PUNK: No no no no. Hey. (to the Bot, a shape in the dark) Hi. Hey. You're my nine o'clock.
Silence. CU the Bot's CRT face: a single flat line.
2. EXIT PUNK: I'll count floors. (beat) We're not moving. (beat) You know it's a good sign when the elevator dies and you're in here with the guy who's paid to hit you.
Bot: nothing. Reaction CU: Exit Punk waits for a laugh that doesn't come.
3. Handheld, circling the small room as he tries things.
EXIT PUNK: Do you like it? Fighting? (silence) I don't mind the fighting. It's the thing before. The waiting. First round I threw up in a bucket. Please don't tell anyone, they'll put it on a poster.
Insert: the CRT face flickers to a small, polite bucket icon. Exit Punk: "Was that a joke? Was that you?" Nothing.
4. He gives up and slides down the wall. A long deliberate silence, eight seconds, the red light pulsing. The camera slowly pushes in on him as his shoulders drop. For the first time he stops talking.
5. Faint sound. Lo-fi, off-key: someone humming the hold music from the building's phone line. CU Exit Punk's eyes move. WIDE reveal: it's coming from the Bot, who has been humming the entire time, under everything.
EXIT PUNK: You've been humming this whole time.
SHUTUP BOT: Fine.
EXIT PUNK: (a dazed laugh) Okay. Okay. Keep going.
6. They sit side by side. The hum, now with Exit Punk joining in, flat and wrong. CRT face: a small smile of two pixels.
7. The elevator jolts back to life. The doors open on blinding stage light and a roaring crowd. The Bot stands first, offers a hand and pulls him up.
EXIT PUNK: I'm going to lose to you so hard.
SHUTUP BOT: Fine.
8. REACTION CU Exit Punk: grinning for the first time in the video. Hard cut to black.
