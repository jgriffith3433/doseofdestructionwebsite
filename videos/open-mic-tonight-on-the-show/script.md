# Open Mic (tonight on the show)

Midnight. The Foundry furnace floor rises and becomes a stage; spotlights slam on; the whole city watches on screens.
THE ORCHESTRATOR (host, theatrical, conducting the lights with a baton): Good evening, city! Tonight on Dose of Destruction... a walk-on. No sponsor. No record. One skateboard.
(Spotlight on an empty entrance. A beat. Nothing. Then the scrape of wheels: Exit Punk rolls in, slow, one foot on the board, hands in his pockets. The crowd doesn't know whether to cheer.)
EXIT PUNK: I'm not here for the show.
(He kicks the board up into his hand.)
EXIT PUNK: I'm here for the exit.
(Across the stage, on City Top, his opponent Kaelen lands hard and the floor cracks. The Orchestrator grins and raises the baton.)
THE ORCHESTRATOR: Then you'll have to go through the main event.
(Exit Punk drops the board, wheels down. FIGHT! A rush of the fight: a dodge, the board swung, a hit that sends Kaelen flying, the crowd erupting.)
EXIT PUNK (catching his breath, dry): Ratings went up?
THE ORCHESTRATOR: Eleven points.
