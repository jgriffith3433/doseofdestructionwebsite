# Open Mic (tonight on the show): story

**Logline.** A TV host sells a nobody to the whole city as a joke; the nobody refuses to play along, says he's only here to leave, and then wins so convincingly the host can only report the ratings.

**Orchestrator** wants a spectacle: he conducts the lights, loves the show, sees the walk-on as content. **Exit Punk** wants out: he won't perform, won't smile, answers a speech with five words. **Kaelen** is the main event: he lands, the floor cracks, he's the show's answer.

**Turn:** "I'm here for the exit": the crowd doesn't know whether to cheer, and the host decides the refusal is the best act of the night.
**Payoff:** the fight, then the dry "Ratings went up?" and the host's flat "Eleven points": the kid who refuses to perform is the best television the show has had.

**Why it matters:** Season 1's opening (Foundry floor becomes a stage at midnight, "a walk-on, no sponsor, no record, one skateboard", "I'm not here for the show, I'm here for the exit", Ratings eleven points) in 35 seconds.
**Who it's for:** anyone who knows what a talent show host and an unimpressed teenager are. No game knowledge needed.
